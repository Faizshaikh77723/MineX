About MineX Intelligence

MineX was engineered under problem statement SIH26023 to provide high-reliability, verifiable AI synthesis for critical mining governance.

Technical Stack

  • Embeddings: BAAI/bge-m3 (Dense-sparse multilingual semantic vectors)
  • Vector Store: ChromaDB with isolated metadata filtering
  • Language Model: Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct (Fine-tuned via QLoRA on T4 GPU)
  • Inference Engine: Local Ollama runtime in 4-bit quantization on Windows
  • Gateway: High-performance FastAPI with sliding conversational memory

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does MineX differ from public tools like ChatGPT?

MineX is 100% sovereign and air-gapped. No proprietary government documents are transmitted over the public internet. Furthermore, MineX uses a custom QLoRA adapter trained to cite sources and reject answering when evidence is missing.

2. How does the system avoid hallucinations?

The model is constrained by strict prompt boundaries and fine-tuned on negative examples where insufficient context forces the output: "I cannot determine this from my database."

3. What type of queries require a full report?

Adding words like "report", "detailed", "summary", or "analyze" to your query will automatically activate the Autonomous Reporting Engine, producing structured Executive Summaries and source citations.
